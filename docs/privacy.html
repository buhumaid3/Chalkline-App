Chalkline privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Chalkline is a party game. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or any identifier that links back to you.

What stays on your device

Your settings, game history, statistics and custom decks are saved on your device.

Optional sync

If sync is available in the app, your statistics, recent game history and custom decks are sent to our database (hosted by Supabase) under a random sync code created on your device. We store only a one-way fingerprint of that code, not the code itself. This data is not linked to your identity and is used only to show the same progress on your other devices. Anyone who has your sync code can see and add to that progress, so keep it private.

The team and player names you type are stored in game history. Avoid typing information you don't want stored.

Motion sensor

The game reads the motion sensor only during a turn, to detect when you tilt the phone. Motion data never leaves your device.

Children

Chalkline is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Erase progress to clear data on your device. To delete synced data, contact us with your sync code and we will remove it.
