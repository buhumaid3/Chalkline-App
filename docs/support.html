Chalkline support

Chalkline is a charades game for game night: one player holds the phone to their forehead while everyone else acts out, hums or describes the word.

Tilting doesn't register, or up and down are swapped

Open Settings > Test tilt. Hold the phone on your forehead, sideways, screen facing out, then nod down and up. If down and up are swapped, set Tilt direction to Reversed. If it's too jumpy or too stiff, change Tilt sensitivity. You can always switch to tap buttons in Settings > Answering.

The game asks me to turn the phone sideways

Turns are played sideways and the screen stays locked that way until the turn ends. To play upright (for example on a phone stand), tap "Play upright instead", or set Settings > Orientation to "Any way up".

I closed the app in the middle of a turn

Open it again and tap Resume game. Your score, cards and time left are kept.

My progress on another device

Progress is saved on your device. Chalkline has no accounts.
