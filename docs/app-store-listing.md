- **Description:**

Chalkline is charades for game night. One player holds the phone to their forehead, everyone else acts out, hums or describes the word, and the player tilts the phone down for Got it or up to Pass. No ads, no purchases, no account.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Phone on your forehead, sideways, screen facing out
• Tilt down when you guess it, tilt up to pass, or use big tap buttons
• Three right in a row doubles your points, six in a row triples them
• Every card shows how to clue it: act it, describe it, one word, hum it or sounds only. The harder styles score a bonus point

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: guess as many as you can before the timer runs out
• Sudden death: no passing, one pass ends your turn
• Relay: every three right answers, the phone moves to the next teammate

5,685 WORDS IN 18 DECKS
• Animals, food and drink, movies, act it out, jobs, places, sports, characters, around the house, music and dance, nature and science, and a deck for little kids
• UAE & Gulf life and Celebrations
• Four decks in Arabic: animals, food and drink, act it out, and Gulf life
• Easy, medium and hard words, and decks don't repeat until you've seen them all
• Make your own decks for inside jokes and family names, in English or Arabic

A REAL GAME NIGHT
• A chalk scoreboard between turns
• End-of-night highlights: fastest guess, longest run, best turn and the one that got away
• A daily deck: the same 40 cards for everyone, every day
• Team badges, stats, game history and 15 awards
• Four boards to unlock by playing: classroom chalkboard, diner corkboard, garden party and desert-night majlis

BUILT FOR THE ROOM
• Big, high-contrast words you can read from across the room
• The screen stays on and locked sideways during a turn
• Close the app mid-turn and pick up exactly where you left off
• Works offline
• Reduce Motion and tap-only play supported

- **Notes:**

Chalkline is a party game with no account or sign-in.

To play: tap New game, then Start game, then Ready. Turn the phone sideways when asked. In a real game the guesser holds the phone to their forehead, screen facing out, and tilts it down (Got it) or up (Pass) while others give clues.

To review without tilting: Settings > Answering > Tap buttons shows on-screen Got it and Pass buttons. Settings > Orientation > Any way up skips the turn-sideways prompt.

The app works fully offline and collects no data.
