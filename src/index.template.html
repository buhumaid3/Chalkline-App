Chalkline

Charades for game night. Phone on your forehead, everyone else shouting.

How to play

One player holds the phone to their forehead, screen facing out, turned sideways. Everyone else acts, hums or describes the word without saying it.

Tilt the phone down when you guess it. Tilt it up to pass. If tilting doesn't suit your phone case or holder, turn on tap buttons in Settings.

Scoring

Easy cards are worth 1, medium 2, hard 3. Three right in a row doubles each card's points, six in a row triples them. The string lights show your run.

Modes

Classic. Guess as many as you can before the timer runs out.

Sudden death. No passing. The first pass ends your turn, so every card counts.

Relay. After every 3 right answers the phone moves to the next teammate. The clock keeps running.

Clue styles

With clue styles on, each card tells the clue-givers how to clue it: act it, describe it, one word, hum it, or sounds only. One word, humming and sounds are harder, so those cards score 1 extra point.

Arabic decks

Four decks are in Arabic: animals, food and drink, act it out, and Gulf life. Pick them in Choose decks, or tap العربية there to see only Arabic decks. Your own decks can be in Arabic too.

Fixing mistakes

After each turn you'll see every card. Tap a card to flip it between right and passed before the score is added.
